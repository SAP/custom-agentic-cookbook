# Third-party notice materials — draft

`THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt` preserves original notices for the known wheel source
components. `SOURCE-REFERENCES.json` records public upstream locations and exact
checksums. `rust/1.98.1/` and `rust/1.99.0/` contain complete original official
standard-library copyright material and the original root copyright/licenses.

The material conservatively includes known source components beyond the subset
that may be linked. Inclusion does not impose their terms on unrelated work or
claim exact runtime build correspondence. Upstream terms and alternatives remain
unchanged. Final distribution selection and placement are pending review.
